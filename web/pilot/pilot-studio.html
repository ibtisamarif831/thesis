Create and manage desktop icon-recognition pilots from saved reviewer selections.

Set up a pilot

  1. Open Submitted icon sets, enter your private results access key, and select Refresh results.
  2. Choose a saved reviewer dataset with exactly 20 icons and select Create pilot.
  3. Review the icons, name the pilot, and review the fixed sizes (6, 8, 10, 12 and 14 pixels).
  4. Use Copy participant link to share the invitation. Use the separate Pilot results tab to view results, export responses, close the pilot, or delete a closed pilot and its results.

Taking part

Participants open the invitation provided by the study administrator on a desktop or laptop. Each session includes three practice trials, 100 randomized recorded trials, and optional reflections. A target appears for 1.5 seconds, followed by five numbered options at an assigned size. Participants answer using keys 1–5 or the mouse.

This pilot checks task design and usability. Its results do not establish validation of the seven computational features.
